Weapons & Cyberware

Your gear degrades. Your chrome wears down. Your HUD has to be earned.

Environmental Weapon Wear

Weapons in PunkCity are now dynamically affected by shifting weather conditions. The impact is subtle, but there is a real chance your gun jams mid-firefight during a torrential downpour or a blinding sandstorm. The environment around you will actively dictate how reliably your gear operates.

It won't happen every fight. But it will happen at the worst possible moment. Plan for it.

High-Stakes Combat Balance

There are hidden multipliers specific to every weapon type, but the headline is this: the list intentionally rewards high-risk playstyles. Shotguns and melee weapons punch well above their weight class.

Bringing a knife to a gunfight isn't such a stupid idea anymore. If you have the skill to close the gap, you will melt faces.

Volatile Cyberware

Your chrome is no longer a set-and-forget passive stat boost. Cyberware is now inherently volatile and wears down over time. Keep a close eye on your components, either through your system menus or by visiting a ripperdoc for a proper diagnosis.

Neglecting your hardware mid-campaign is the kind of mistake you only make once.

Earn Your HUD

When you first wake up in Night City, your UI is completely missing. This is not a glitch. Vital HUD elements are hardlocked behind specific cyberware. Your Kiroshi optics have up to 8 lens slots, and each one you purchase and install unlocks a different piece of the interface. Until you do, you are flying completely blind.

Head to a ripperdoc, browse their trade menu, and start building your HUD piece by piece.
