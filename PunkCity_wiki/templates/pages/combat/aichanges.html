AI Changes

You are no longer the apex predator by default.

Enemy AI has been completely overhauled, and the changes make the world feel so much more immersive. NPCs no longer stand in the open and take bullets to the face. They take cover aggressively, initiate quickhacks more often, and actively try to disable both you and your tools.

Combat Behavior

The biggest shift is how reactive enemies are. They are no longer passive targets waiting for you to pick them off. Expect flanking, suppressive fire, and coordinated pushes that will punish you for staying stationary too long.

Netrunners

If a netrunner starts cooking your brain, deal with them immediately. Toss a grenade, shoot them, do whatever it takes. Letting them work uninterrupted is a fast way to die.

Faction Accuracy

Enemy accuracy has been tuned to reflect faction lore. Not every enemy is going to shoot like a trained soldier, because not every enemy is one.

  • A Militech squad shoots with discipline and precision. Treat them accordingly.
  • Scavs hopped up on chrome are dangerous up close but sloppy at range.
  • Every faction sits somewhere on that spectrum. Learn the differences.

Stealth

Ghosting through a compound with zero stealth investment is no longer viable. The AI will spot you fast, and going in blind without at least a few points in the core Cool perks is going to end badly.

More importantly, enemies will never fully settle once they have detected a threat. Finding a security breach and casually returning to patrol two minutes later? That does not happen here.

Why should they forget? Finding a dead body and shrugging it off is not immersion. It's a broken game.

Speaking of dead bodies: they now cause immediate caution in nearby NPCs. A corpse dropping next to a guard is not something they ignore just because you stayed quiet.

The Bottom Line

There is a lot more to cover here, and this page will grow as the wiki develops. But the core takeaway is simple: from here on out, you are the one who needs to exercise caution. Play like the city is watching. Because it is.
