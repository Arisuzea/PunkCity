Damage Changes

A fully custom damage model. Your DPS tooltip is lying to you.

The damage system has been completely overhauled, specifically utilizing a customized version of the Damage Scaling & Rebalance mod. A huge shout-out and thank you to RMK and Q for the incredible foundation.

Every hit you land passes through a chain of multipliers before final damage is applied. The order matters. Each step feeds into the next.

A Warning About Tooltips

Because of how this custom model calculates damage under the hood, you can no longer trust the in-game weapon tooltips. In practice, your actual performance will generally feel somewhere between 20% and 40% stronger than what the display screen claims.

And remember, we have an armor system in place that mitigates damage, this also applies to npcs. Wear down their armor and you'll see bigger damage numbers.

Don't Trust the DPS Number

The on-screen DPS figure does not account for the active multipliers in this system. Treat it as a rough base damage guideline only, and trust the actual body count instead. Work is underway to make the UI reflect these multipliers accurately.
