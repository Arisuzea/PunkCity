Resource Scarcity

Your healing items and grenades do not recharge. Plan accordingly.

In PunkCity, you'll find that healing items and grenades no longer magically recharge. This is not a bug; it is completely intentional.

Grenades & Consumables

You now have to carefully consider your loadout before heading into danger. Which specific grenades do you need, how many should you carry, and when is the right time to use them? These resources are no longer infinite or weightless.

With a strict carry limit in place, you are forced to actually plan your approach instead of just hoarding items.

Carry Limit
Vanilla

Bottomless pockets. Carry every grenade type in bulk, pop a medkit whenever, and never think twice about resources.

PunkCity

A hard carry limit means every slot in your loadout is a deliberate choice. No more hoarding. Pick what you need and commit to it.

Ammunition

Ammo capacity has also been cut down significantly, and for good reason. Your enemies can be taken down with just a few well-placed bullets, so letting you carry an infinite stockpile would completely trivialize the combat system. Every shot has to count.

Ammo Economy
Vanilla

Thousands of rounds on hand at all times. Spray and pray is always an option. Resource management is effectively nonexistent.

PunkCity

Reduced ammo caps force you to shoot with intention. Enemies go down fast when you aim well, so wasting shots is a choice you'll regret.

How to Survive

If you want to survive, get used to scouting your targets and planning your angles. Rushing in blind will drain your supplies in seconds and leave you with nothing when it matters most.

Every engagement is a resource transaction. Make sure you're coming out ahead.

Best of luck out there, choom.
