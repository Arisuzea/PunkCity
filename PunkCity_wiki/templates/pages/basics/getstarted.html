Getting Started

Personal advice on surviving your first few hours in PunkCity.

PunkCity is a brutal list, built with Very Hard difficulty in mind. But it's also incredibly rewarding. It pays off to scout your environment and plan your next move. Your strategy doesn't need to be in-depth. Even short-term positioning helps.

"I'll shoot this guy first, wait for that gonk to run up the stairs, and pace myself from there."

The game rewards tactical thinking and heavily punishes carelessness. Here's some personal advice to help you find your footing.

The Financial Kickstart

When starting a new playthrough, the first thing to do is take out a loan of around 200k to 300k. Use that initial capital to buy decent cyberware and gear right away.

It's possible to pay-to-win through Virtual Atelier, but keep in mind that doing so is financially punishing. If buying your way to success feels too cheap, hit the NCPD scanners instead. They offer a decent payout and are repeatable, but every time you repeat them the difficulty spikes.

Heads Up

Repeating NCPD scanners will keep scaling the difficulty. Don't grind them mindlessly or you'll hit a wall fast.

Early-Game Loadout

In the early game, running an Assault Rifle, a Shotgun, and a Throwing Knife is highly recommended. Here's why each one earns its slot.

Assault Rifle
Role

Your main workhorse for mid-range firefights. Keep this in hand for most engagements.

Throwing Knife
Role

Essential if you want to attempt stealth. Just know that stealth at low levels is incredibly tough. You need a baseline of at least 10 Cool to avoid getting detected the moment you step behind an enemy.

Shotgun
Role

Your panic option. Enemies using melee weapons are heavily buffed in this list and will absolutely destroy you if they close the distance. Keep this equipped and ready. Trust.

Forge Your Own Path

Once you get comfortable and build up your wealth, start branching out into your actual desired build. PunkCity is not a rigid fixed-path modlist. There are countless ways to build a legendary merc. What's outlined here is just one of them.

Get out there and explore, choom.
