What's PunkCity

A Wabbajack modlist built around one idea: the city owes you nothing.

Overview

PunkCity is a Wabbajack modlist designed to deliver a brutal, grounded cyberpunk experience. Think of Ghost of Tsushima's lethal difficulty — that's the baseline. This is a world where exploration is heavily rewarded and interactivity is everywhere.

The world doesn't scale to match your level. Instead, you have to match the world through sheer grit and effort.

How It Plays

To survive, you'll need to use every tool at your disposal. Fully realized banking systems and stock markets offer high-stakes ways to fund your mercenary lifestyle. But remember — you start as a nobody, bound by the same harsh rules as everyone else.

Street cred matters. The city's elite establishments won't let you through the door without the right reputation or social standing.

  • Even a routine carjacking can instantly spiral into a high-speed NCPD chase
  • Lose the cops and the ride is yours — sell it, keep it, or hack the security system yourself
  • A living economy with banking, stock markets, and chop shops to interact with
  • The world is dense with secrets — this wiki exists because you'll need it

Who It's For

PunkCity is not a power fantasy. If you want to feel unstoppable from hour one, this isn't your list. If you want a city that pushes back, systems that demand your attention, and a world that rewards patience and curiosity — you're in the right place.

If you're ready for an unforgiving, deeply immersive challenge, hop in and join the community in PunkCity.

Key Features at a Glance

  • Lethal, grounded combat — every fight is a real threat
  • Banking & stock market — fund your lifestyle through high-stakes systems
  • Street cred & reputation — the city gatekeeps its best until you've earned it
  • Dynamic NCPD — the law responds realistically to your actions
  • Deep netrunning — hack vehicles, breach systems, own the grid
  • Rich interactivity — a wiki isn't optional, it's practically essential

Before you start

This list is thoroughly tested and optimized to be as stable and crash-free as possible. However, modding is a wild beast, and it still has its bugs and quirks. Get into the habit of saving your game often.

The vanilla tutorial sequence is completely disabled in favor of this wiki. If you go into the game expecting a smooth, hand-holding tutorial experience, prepare to be disappointed. Read the documentation first.

Some inputs and keybinds in the list aren’t explicitly displayed on screen yet. I’m currently learning how to improve the UI for that, but for now, consider yourself warned.

Finally, this modlist is constantly in development for as long as I choose to support it. I always welcome constructive suggestions and feedback, but unconstructive whining won't get you very far.
