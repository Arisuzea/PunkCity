Vanilla vs PunkCity

What's actually different, and why it matters.

Vanilla can still be compelling, but it often feels incomplete when it comes to truly pulling you into Night City. PunkCity fills those gaps, from minute details like a needs system all the way to grander overhauls of the core combat and gameplay loop.

Exploration
Vanilla

Low-level exploration rewards you with garbage common loot. The game deems you unworthy, so you are. Makes you want to rush the main story and never look back.

PunkCity

Watson in Act 1 actually feels rewarding. You can feel yourself getting stronger but you have to put in the effort to get there.

Housing
Vanilla

Apartments feel permanent in the worst way. Buy once, forget forever. You pay taxes on everything else but somehow rent doesn't exist, unimmersive.

PunkCity

Eviction Notice forces you to actually pay rent to keep a roof over your head. Miss a payment and find out, or rent a motel.

Consequences
Vanilla

Kill all the civilians you want. Your street cred doesn't care. The city doesn't care. Nobody cares. Great.

PunkCity

Killing in cold blood is heavily punishable and incredibly costly. Your reputation is on the line every time you pull the trigger. Yikes.

Vehicle Theft
Vanilla

See a cool car, steal it GTA-style, finish a gig, walk back out & it's gone. You never truly own anything you take.

PunkCity

A proper theft system lets you take a stolen ride to a chop shop or hack the security system yourself. The choice is yours, choom.

Street Vendors
Vanilla

Those standing street vendors? Scammers. Won't sell you a thing. Completely decorative. Very unimmersive, thank you CD Projekt.

PunkCity

Any vendor you see will actually sell you something. It might not be luxury dining, but it's real. Don't complain.
