Patch Notes
  • New: Blunt weapons can knock out enemies, with a chance of fatality remaining (careful on pacifist quests).
  • New: Wiki added to the list. Open the HUB Menu and click Databases on the bottom right.
  • New: More ways to earn money: online betting and gambling on Dede Games via your computer.
  • New: Grenades can be cooked, and can overcook and explode in your hand.
  • New: Weapon Patches (EXPERIMENTAL)
  • New: Threadscape (EXPERIMENTAL)
  • Changed: All weapon attachments are back to how the default game made them (quality, slot and gun type are respected).
  • Changed: Significantly fewer modded vehicles, with more to be added in future updates.
  • Changed: Stealth rebalance: a perk is now needed to get proficient stealth results.
  • Changed: Attachments that guarantee a crit when undetected are more significant (enemies made squishier).
  • Changed: Stealthrunner's optional objectives are now hidden.
  • Changed: Better parkour system: more of what looks climbable is now climbable.
  • Changed: Enhanced driving experience, cars feel a lot closer to the FH series.
  • Changed: Guns are guns: a bullet to the head now deals significantly more damage.
  • Changed: Enemies can now move and shoot while a quickhack is uploading.
  • Changed: Leveling and Reputation now scale on how many gigs you complete. NCPD Scanners no longer give either and are now just good for money.
  • Changed: Removed hackshops for stolen cars, as they conflict with other systems.
  • Changed: Removed vendor filters that limit what you can sell to specific vendors, as they were buggy and could break certain systems.
  • Changed: Empty containers no longer refill over time, to give a sense of progression for completionists. May become optional in the future.
  • Changed: Separators are further organized.
  • Fixed: Invisible cigars (for Eve & Meredith).
  • Fixed: Unable to sell certain gear.
  • Fixed: Performance issues caused by too many NPCs existing at once.
  • Fixed: Various other small issues, mostly for consistency.

Patch Notes
  • Combat has been made noticeably more difficult, headshots are now far more rewarding, while poor aim carries a real penalty.
  • Stealth has been made easier. Vanilla-style sneaking is back, with the only change being that your surroundings now react to noise from related actions.
  • Thrown items (excluding Legendary and Iconic weapons) must now be manually retrieved after use. Super immersive.
  • Hacking's time dilation now runs on a "Dilation" bar instead of the previous RAM-based system.
  • Modded vehicles are now exclusive to the player and will no longer spawn randomly out in traffic.

  • Injury mechanics are now handled by Digital Vixen's Injuries mod.
