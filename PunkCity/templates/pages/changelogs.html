Patch Notes
  • Combat has been made noticeably more difficult, headshots are now far more rewarding, while poor aim carries a real penalty.
  • Stealth has been made easier. Vanilla-style sneaking is back, with the only change being that your surroundings now react to noise from related actions.
  • Thrown items (excluding Legendary and Iconic weapons) must now be manually retrieved after use. Super immersive.
  • Hacking's time dilation now runs on a "Dilation" bar instead of the previous RAM-based system.
  • Modded vehicles are now exclusive to the player and will no longer spawn randomly out in traffic.
  • Starting a new game now grants starting weapons based on your chosen Lifepath, making the intro questline far more manageable, running it with just a starter pistol on Very Hard proved significantly harder than intended.
  • Injury mechanics are now handled by Digital Vixen's Injuries mod.
  • Ammo counts have been significantly increased across the board.
  • Fixers are now engaged through dedicated quests rather than unsolicited calls, much more immersive than the old telemarketer routine.
  • Reloading must now be triggered manually.
  • Careless reloading is now penalized. For example, reloading with 25/30 rounds remaining will discard those 25 rounds instead of folding them into the new magazine.
  • True First Person introduces a range of changes, including leaning, and adds meaningful incentive to run the "Aimassist" optic mod, which previously felt unnecessary for most playstyles.

Patch Notes
  • Gig rewards and gun prices have been increased by 40–60% and 100–150% respectively. Guns should be a luxury, something afforded by gangs and a lucky few. You don't really see that many civilians strapped, do ya?
  • The combat experience has been overhauled to feel a bit more arcadey, but still realistic enough to be reasonable. Balancing high-damage combat without the proper tools is harder than imagined, but don't worry that concept will be revisited soon.
  • Reworked the entire exploration-reward dynamic. Legendary items are still obtainable but significantly rarer, and not dramatically stronger than other tiers, leveling up will be necessary to unlock their full potential.
  • The "you shouldn't be here" feel has been toned down. Combat will feel more equal whilst still being lethal.
  • Stealth has been buffed slightly, grabbing someone for a stealth takedown should now work more reliably than before.
  • Being rescued by Trauma Team should now also repair your Cyberware. Wohoo immersion!
  • Crafting by your car stash now needs the trunk to be open.
